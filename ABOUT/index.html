Jinhua Funbelle Toys is a specialized manufacturing factory focused on TPR toys and Putty Slime for mid-to-high-end brands, professional buyers, and long-term international partners. We are not positioned as a low-cost commodity supplier. We build our reputation on material performance, formula stability, production details, batch consistency, and compliant delivery.

Built for Quality-Focused Buyers

Jinhua Funbelle Toys focuses on TPR toys and Putty Slime for the mid-to-high-end market. We do not compete on low prices or commodity-grade production. Instead, we invest in the areas that matter to professional buyers: material feel, formulation stability, manufacturing consistency, packaging presentation, and quality control.

From product development and sampling to mass production and pre-shipment inspection, we work closely with brand owners and sourcing teams to deliver products that are stable, inspection-ready, and suitable for international markets.

Strict Internal Quality Control, Supported by Third-Party Inspection

Jinhua Funbelle Toys implements full-process quality management covering incoming materials, formulation, production, finished products, packaging, and retained samples. Our internal quality control is strict by design, because mid-to-high-end products require consistency — not just a good sample.

In addition, we cooperate with China Quality Service, a reputable third-party inspection company in China, to conduct third-party inspection and sampling according to international buyers’ requirements and target-market compliance standards. Through incoming inspection, in-process inspection, finished-product inspection, AQL sampling, inspection reports, and corrective-action follow-up, we help ensure that products meet buyer requirements in materials, appearance, function, packaging, labeling, and compliance — reducing cross-border sourcing risk.

From Concept to Stable Mass Production

We support OEM/ODM cooperation and work with customers throughout formula communication, color and scent customization, packaging solutions, sampling, mass production, and pre-shipment inspection. Whether you need a customized TPR toy program or a Putty Slime formulation with specific hand feel and packaging requirements, we help turn the concept into a stable, repeatable product.
